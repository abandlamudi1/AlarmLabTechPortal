Role At A Glance

The QE Lab Tech role focuses on preparing and maintaining hardware infrastructure that powers QE automation efforts.

  • Solder relay boards, automation devices, camera assemblies, and support wiring.
  • Assemble modular "kits" that reproduce real-world device setups for testing.
  • Research and prototype new hardware-to-software integrations to expand automation coverage.
